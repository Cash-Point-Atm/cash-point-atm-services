Questions & Answers

Every question you have.
Answered honestly.

Do I have to manage or maintain anything?

No. Not one single thing. Installation, cash loading, maintenance, monitoring, repairs, and customer support are all our responsibility — permanently. Your staff doesn't touch the machine and doesn't field machine-related complaints.

How exactly do I get paid?

You earn a percentage of the surcharge fee from every withdrawal made at your ATM. We send you a detailed monthly income report that breaks down transaction volume, revenue generated, and your exact earnings. It's completely transparent.

What does this cost me?

Nothing. Zero dollars. Not for the machine, not for the installation, not for maintenance, not for cash loading, not for monitoring. The entire operation is funded and managed by Cash Point ATM Services. You provide the location — we cover everything else.

What if the ATM breaks down?

We fix it — at no cost to you and on our timeline. All service calls and repairs are fully our responsibility. You don't call a repair company, you don't pay a service fee, and you don't coordinate anything. We handle it from start to finish.

How much will I actually earn?

Your monthly earnings depend on your foot traffic and transaction volume. A busy bar or convenience store will earn more than a low-traffic boutique. Call or text Chris — he'll give you a realistic estimate based on your specific location, not a best-case scenario.

Is there a contract or long-term commitment?

We'll walk you through the placement agreement before anything is installed. There are no hidden clauses and no tricks. The goal is a long-term relationship that works for both parties — not locking you into something that doesn't.

What if I want the ATM removed?

We'll have a conversation. Our goal is placements that make sense for everyone involved. If your circumstances change, we'll work through it together. We're a locally owned operation — this isn't a faceless corporate arrangement.

Do you handle the compliance and regulation side?

Yes. ATM placement involves regulatory requirements that vary by state and municipality. We handle all of it. You don't need to research ADA compliance, surcharge disclosures, or machine certification. That's our area of expertise.
