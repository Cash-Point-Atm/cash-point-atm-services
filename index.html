Common Questions

Everything You Want To Know

Do I have to manage anything?

Absolutely not. We handle installation, cash loading, maintenance, repairs, and monitoring. Your only job is to let us place the machine.

How do I get paid?

You earn a percentage of the surcharge fee from every transaction. We provide monthly reports so you always know exactly what you're earning.

What does it cost me?

Nothing. Zero. Not for installation, not for maintenance, not for cash loading, not ever. The entire operation is covered by us.

Is there any risk or downside?

No liability, no maintenance responsibility, no operational workload. You provide floor space you already have — we provide everything else.

How much will I earn?

It depends on your foot traffic and transaction volume. Call or text Chris and he can give you a real estimate based on your specific location.

What if the ATM breaks?

We fix it. Period. All repairs and service calls are fully covered at no cost to you. If it needs cash, we load it. If it breaks, we fix it.
